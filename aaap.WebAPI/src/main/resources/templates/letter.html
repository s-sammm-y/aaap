To My Favourite Person ❤️

My love,

I don't think I say this enough, but having you in my life is one of the most beautiful things that has ever happened to me.

Sometimes I think about all the little moments we've shared. The random conversations, the silly jokes, the moments where we did absolutely nothing and still somehow had the best time.

And those are the moments I treasure the most.

You have become such an important part of my life that imagining my days without you feels impossible. You make ordinary days feel special just by being there.

I know we won't always have perfect days. We'll have disagreements, bad moods, stressful days and moments where things don't go exactly the way we want.

But through all of that, I want you to know one thing: I will always choose you.

I want to make more memories with you. More stupid jokes. More late-night conversations. More adventures. More photographs. More little moments that we'll look back at years from now and smile about.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being a part of my life. And thank you for giving me so many reasons to smile.

Here's to us, to everything we've already shared, and to everything that's still waiting for us.

I love you. ❤️

Yours always,
